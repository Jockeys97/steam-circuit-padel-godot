# Timing presentation in 3D — the ring, the words, the bar

- Status: open
- Type: task
- Mode: AFK
- Owner: crew-timinghud
- Blocked by: none

Slice S14c. The reference draws the timing around the controlled athlete; the port
draws the words in a corner panel and nothing on the field.

## Objective

Bring the reference's timing presentation onto the court: the arc/ring around the
**active athlete** while a shot is charging, filling toward the perfect window and
turning when the window is met; the advice word and the profile colour next to it;
the precision bar; and the energy bar, which the reference draws **under the active
athlete** while the port shows it in the HUD panel.

**Both halves, confirmed by the owner on 2026-09-17** after being asked which of the
two he meant by *"le scritte e le logiche del timing con Perfetto"*: the charging
guide **and** the verdict. The verdict is the second half and it is not the same
element: when the shot lands, the reference writes the timing grade in words — the
locale's `shotPerfect` ("PERFETTO") plus `good` / `early` / `late` — **above the
athlete who struck**, in the grade's own colour, fading over 0.28 s
(`js/render.js:1041-1065`). The port has the same grade and the same word but shows
them only in the bottom-centre panel (`godot/game/hud.gd:426-430`), so on the field
there is no verdict at all. A timing you cannot see is a timing you cannot learn.

The measurement discipline is part of the deliverable: a marker that exists in the
code and cannot be found in a frame is not delivered. The active-athlete zone and pin
landed this way and their numbers are in
`docs/wayfinder/evidence/active-player-marker.md` — read it before sizing anything.

## Inputs and outputs

In: `state.shotRead`, `state.shotCharge`, the active athlete's position, the camera.
Out: rendered evidence frames plus a measurement of the mark in them, and a slice
section that fails if the ring stops tracking the charge.

## Tests

A `_timing_presentation` section in the slice test: the ring node exists, is
invisible when nothing is charging and visible while charging, its fill tracks
`state.shotCharge`/`shotRead.eta` in the direction the reference uses, and the advice
word matches `shotAdvice_<advice>`. Same section, the verdict: the label node exists,
is hidden when `state.shotFeedback` is null and visible when it is not, sits on
`state.shotFeedback.paddleKey`'s athlete — **the striker, not the newly active
athlete** — and carries the word and colour of that feedback's grade. Plus the frame
evidence below.

## Execution commands

```bash
GODOT=/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot
$GODOT --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1280x720 --path godot/ res://game/Match.tscn \
  -- --capture=match --camera=default --tier=3 --seed=20260916
python3 godot/game/tools/inspect_png.py godot/game/out/rally.png --region=ring@x,y,w,h
python3 godot/game/tools/yellow_map.py godot/game/out/rally.png yellow "x,y,w,h" 4
$GODOT --headless --path godot/ --script res://tests/game_slice_test.gd
```

A capture that renders a whole match takes ~3 minutes; for iteration write a
throwaway `SceneTree` probe that renders one frame (~10 s) and delete it afterwards.
