# Arena art direction

- Status: open
- Type: grilling
- Mode: HITL
- Owner: unassigned
- Blocked by: [Camera and feel spike](camera-and-feel-spike.md)

## Question

Nine arenas (officina, locomotive, clockwork, cattedrale, forgia, tempesta,
abissale, caldera, orrery) plus the locomotive backdrop. Decide which are rebuilt
as geometry in Godot, which reuse the existing painted artwork as textures or
skyboxes, and which — if any — go through the Meshy lane. Also settle what makes
them read as nine distinct places once the fake perspective is gone.

## Why it matters

Arena route is a large, expensive choice and it depends on what 3D framing the
camera spike proves. Deciding it before the spike risks rebuilding art that the
new camera makes unnecessary.

## Resolved when

A per-arena decision with the production route named, and a first arena built in
the spike scene as proof.
