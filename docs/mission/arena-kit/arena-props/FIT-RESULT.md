## 7. Deviations and judgement calls (numbered so they are easy to review or revert)

1. **`tools/arena-kit/previz_2d.py` — one derived assertion moved.** It refused to draw (`exit 2`,
   `depth … outside budget 3.260`) once the declared depths are the measured meshes: four slots'
   real depth exceeds the flat constant. The check now bounds a slot's depth by the depth *its own
   anchor* leaves to the glass (plus half a centimetre for the 2 dp table, named `ROUND_TOL`, and
   `FIT_MARGIN = 0.05`). The other nine checks are untouched and the PNGs come from the tool. Both
   alternatives were worse: a stale plan (the plan-fresh gate) or a table that lies about the art.
   Revert = restore the three-line check.
2. **Two aurora anchors were nudged 1 cm after the first pass** (−11.99 → −12.00 for `hero_landmark`,
   −11.02 → −11.03 for `light_source`): the first pass fitted from the 2 dp measured depth, and the
   re-measure showed a 4.8 cm margin on a 5 cm target because the true mesh is up to 5 mm deeper.
   §3's margins are the re-measured ones.
3. **`--presets=default` on the capture run** (5 frames, one per arena, as the gate asks) instead of
   the harness's default of three presets (15 frames). The other presets can be rendered with
   `--presets=default,wide,playable`; the numbers and the fit are the `default` framing.
