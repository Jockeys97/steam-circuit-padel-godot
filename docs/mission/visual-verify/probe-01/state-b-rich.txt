STAT-MARK ROW: under each description there is a row of small marks.
- On cards 1-4 the row contains 4 small square/rectangular marks with visible gaps between them. All 4 marks carry the same ink, a light grey, nearest hex #C0C0C0. No mark of any other colour appears in the row.
- The marks are small relative to the card's text.
- On cards 5 and 6 no mark row is visible.

CLIPPING: cards 5 and 6 are cut off by the bottom edge of the frame - their status word is not visible and their panel ends at the image boundary. Cards 1-4 are fully inside the frame.

DETERMINISTIC FACTS - read from the port's own source, not from the image:
- The mark row is drawn as one text label: five glyphs, an optional number of U+25AE followed by U+25AF to make five ticks in total.
- That label's font colour is overridden from the theme token "stat_bar"; the port's value for that token is #7ee0ff. For rival cards the token is "stat_bar_rival", value #ffb08c.
- The label has text clipping enabled, and the card container clips its content.
- On cards 1-4 the description sentence wraps onto a second line.
