# Racket gallery architecture

Throwaway sketch. Nothing here mounts in Godot.

## Journey

Select a set. Pick a rung. Orbit the stand-in. Set grip, strings, special. Pick a teammate. Send.

State lives in memory. Send is a confirmation, not a network call.

## 3D

CSS rotateY on the real front PNG. Drag to orbit, slow idle spin, reduced-motion kills the idle. Not a GLB. Not Meshy.

## Families (console.html)

Stage: the racket is the product. Coverflow plus a send button on the stage.
Ladder: the five rungs are a magazine story. Inspect desk on the side.
Bench: loadout-first. Filmstrip, quiet model, player chips, Send.

## Non-goals

No Meshy upload, no Godot scene, no payments, no persistence, no real multiplayer.

## Owner gates

Does the orbit feel fast? Is this the right picker? Is Send-to-player the right end of the flow?
